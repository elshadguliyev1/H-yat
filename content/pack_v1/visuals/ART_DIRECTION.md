# Visual direction
Static 2D painterly narrative game. Consistent warm nostalgic light, desaturated blue/ochre palette, natural Azerbaijani apartments, school classrooms, bus stops, rural courtyards. No embedded text, watermarks or logos. Backgrounds 16:9; character portraits 1:1. Keep characters visually consistent across age stages.
